For the love of Styria.

s’Genussplatz’l was born out of joy: joy in meeting people, in good food and drink, and in our green Styrian homeland. We wanted to create a place where cyclists, hikers and day-trippers can arrive, take a deep breath and get a taste of the region.

Because style and detail matter to us, it did not become an ordinary rest stop, but one where copper, glass and wood meet nature, surrounded by art and a large garden.

The walnut tree. It has given shade here for generations. On Styrian farmsteads the walnut tree was often the centre of the yard, and in autumn fresh walnuts and young wine simply belong together.

Roses that never wilt.

One-of-a-kind, hand-forged pieces by a scrap-metal artist from the region and a friend of ours.

Artwork 1 of 7

All around the garden you will come across figures made of metal: roses that never wilt, vines heavy with grapes and a heart made of wine bottles. A scrap-metal artist from the region, a friend of ours, forged them by hand with great attention to detail, giving old iron a second life.

You are welcome to enquire about the piece you would like.

Right on the spa circuit.

Twelve spas, thirteen stages, around 418 kilometres from Bad Waltersdorf to Slovenia.

s’Genussplatz’l is a partner of Bike & Spa, a cross-border cycling project in the Interreg Slovenia–Austria programme. The loop links Bad Waltersdorf, Loipersdorf and Bad Radkersburg with Moravske Toplice, Lendava, Ptuj and Maribor.

The stage from Mureck to Bad Gleichenberg runs right past us on the Gleichenberg cycle route R45, a good spot for a break along the way.
