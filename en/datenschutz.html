Hosting

This website is provided via GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you access the site, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address, the time of access, the page requested and your browser identifier in order to deliver the site and protect it against misuse. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in secure and stable operation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). For transfers to the USA, GitHub relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or on standard contractual clauses.

Visitor statistics with Umami

To see how many people visit the website and which pages are popular, we use Umami. Umami does not set cookies, does not store IP addresses and does not create profiles of individual visitors. It records aggregated information such as the page visited, the referring page, device type, browser and country. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in improving our services (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Fonts and content

Fonts, images, maps and route data are stored on our own web space. When the page loads, no data is transferred to Google Fonts or any other font provider. We draw the maps ourselves from OpenStreetMap data.

Links to other services

We link to Komoot, Google Maps, Instagram, steiermark.com and our partners’ websites. You only leave our site when you click on such a link. The privacy policy of the respective provider then applies.

Contact by email

If you write to us, we use your details only to answer your enquiry, for example about an artwork. We do not pass them on, and we delete them as soon as they are no longer needed and no statutory retention obligations apply.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. Simply write to us at info@s-genussplatzl.at. If you believe that the processing of your data infringes data protection law, you can lodge a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority (Österreichische Datenschutzbehörde), Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Wien, dsb.gv.at.
