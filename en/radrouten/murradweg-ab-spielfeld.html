From Spielfeld along the Mur.

The classic route on the Mur: flat along the river, via Mureck, all the way to us in Unterpurkla.

Distance
approx. 26.9 km
Climbing
hardly any climbs
Start
Spielfeld
Finish
s’Genussplatz’l, Unterpurkla

Along the way

  1. Start

    Spielfeld

    Border town on the Mur, right by the South Styrian Wine Road. This is where the ride downstream begins.

  2. km 14

    Mureck

    The ship mill was set back on the Mur in 1997 after a historic model, one of the last floating ship mills in Central Europe.

  3. km 18

    Gosdorf

    Through the Mur’s riverside meadows, surrounded by the UNESCO Mura-Drava-Danube Biosphere Reserve.

  4. Finish

    s’Genussplatz’l

    A rest beneath the walnut tree with regional drinks, shade, a toilet, a free e-bike charging point and an air-conditioned room for hot days.

Paths: Mur Cycle Path R2, finally the Gleichenberg cycle route R45.

Journey’s end: a rest beneath the walnut tree.

At the end of the route you will find shade beneath the walnut tree, regional drinks, a toilet, free e-bike charging and, on hot days, an air-conditioned room. From March to October, daily from 9 am to 10 pm.
