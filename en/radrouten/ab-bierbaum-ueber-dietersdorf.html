From Bierbaum to the Mur.

The short, flat tour from the north-west: from Bierbaum am Auersbach via Dietersdorf am Gnasbach and Deutsch Goritz to us in Unterpurkla.

Distance
approx. 17.6 km
Climbing
hardly any climbs
Start
Bierbaum am Auersbach
Finish
s’Genussplatz’l, Unterpurkla

Along the way

  1. Start

    Bierbaum am Auersbach

    Village on the Auersbach, the start of the tour.

  2. km 3

    Dietersdorf am Gnasbach

    Village in the Gnasbach valley.

  3. km 10

    Deutsch Goritz

    From here the last few kilometres run across the plain to the Mur.

  4. Finish

    s’Genussplatz’l

    A rest beneath the walnut tree with regional drinks, shade, a toilet, a free e-bike charging point and an air-conditioned room for hot days.

Journey’s end: a rest beneath the walnut tree.

At the end of the route you will find shade beneath the walnut tree, regional drinks, a toilet, free e-bike charging and, on hot days, an air-conditioned room. From March to October, daily from 9 am to 10 pm.
