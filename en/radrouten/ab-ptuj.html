From Ptuj over the hills.

From the town on the Drava through the hill country of the Slovenske gorice to Apače and across the Steirerbrücke to us.

Distance
approx. 46.1 km
Climbing
approx. 170 m
Start
Ptuj
Finish
s’Genussplatz’l, Unterpurkla

Along the way

  1. Start

    Ptuj

    Town on the Drava with a castle above the old town, a stage town of the Bike & Spa loop.

  2. km 28

    Benedikt

    Village in the Slovenske gorice, the hill country between the Drava and the Mur.

  3. km 39

    Apače

    Village on the Slovenian bank of the Mur, the starting point for the bridge to Austria.

  4. km 42

    Steirerbrücke

    Since 2006 this suspension bridge has swung 82.5 metres across the Mur.

  5. Finish

    s’Genussplatz’l

    A rest beneath the walnut tree with regional drinks, shade, a toilet, a free e-bike charging point and an air-conditioned room for hot days.

Paths: Cycle paths and back roads through the Slovenske gorice, Steirerbrücke, finally the Gleichenberg cycle route R45.

Journey’s end: a rest beneath the walnut tree.

At the end of the route you will find shade beneath the walnut tree, regional drinks, a toilet, free e-bike charging and, on hot days, an air-conditioned room. From March to October, daily from 9 am to 10 pm.
