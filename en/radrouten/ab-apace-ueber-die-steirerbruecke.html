From Apače across the Steirerbrücke.

The shortest route comes from Slovenia: across the suspension bridge and through the Mur’s riverside meadows, and you are with us in half an hour.

Distance
approx. 7.2 km
Climbing
hardly any climbs
Start
Apače
Finish
s’Genussplatz’l, Unterpurkla

Along the way

  1. Start

    Apače

    Village on the Slovenian bank of the Mur, the starting point for the bridge to Austria.

  2. km 3

    Steirerbrücke

    Since 2006 this suspension bridge has swung 82.5 metres across the Mur, right in the heart of the UNESCO Mura-Drava-Danube Biosphere Reserve.

  3. Finish

    s’Genussplatz’l

    A rest beneath the walnut tree with regional drinks, shade, a toilet, a free e-bike charging point and an air-conditioned room for hot days.

Paths: Cycle paths on both banks of the Mur, Steirerbrücke, finally the Gleichenberg cycle route R45.

Journey’s end: a rest beneath the walnut tree.

At the end of the route you will find shade beneath the walnut tree, regional drinks, a toilet, free e-bike charging and, on hot days, an air-conditioned room. From March to October, daily from 9 am to 10 pm.
