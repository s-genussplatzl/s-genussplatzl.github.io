E-bike charging by the cycle path.

Charge your battery free of charge while you rest beneath the walnut tree: right on the Gleichenberg cycle route R45, just before the Mur Cycle Path.

Cost
free
Where
Unterpurkla 28, on the Gleichenberg cycle route R45
When
March to October, 9 am to 10 pm
Mur Cycle Path
a few minutes away

Charge up and unwind.

Park your bike at the charging point, plug in your charger and treat yourself to a break. While the battery charges, you can enjoy shade beneath the walnut tree, regional drinks, a toilet and, on hot days, an air-conditioned room.

Please bring your own charger.
